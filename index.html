Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour & Love

Founded in 1999, Sri Shalini Ranjani Caterers has been the most trusted name in traditional South Indian catering across Chennai and Tamil Nadu. What began as a small family kitchen has blossomed into a full-service catering operation serving thousands of guests each year.

We believe every meal is a celebration. Our team of experienced chefs uses authentic recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest ingredients sourced daily from local markets. From intimate family gatherings to grand weddings of 2000+ guests, we bring the same passion and precision to every plate.
